# Chillar: "RIP Goa Plans" Launch Campaign

**How do you get students to save money together, when every group trip dies in the WhatsApp chat with the same cause of death: "broke"?**

## The answer

Make the dead trip the hero of the campaign. Chillar is a UPI round-up savings app for students. Every UPI payment rounds up, the spare change is saved in one 11 PM debit, and friends pool it into shared Squad Jars. "RIP Goa Plans" is a 55-second Instagram Reel set in a graveyard of dead group plans, where an autopsy finds the missing ₹50 a day and the trip comes back to life.

## The insight

Students are less broke than the group chat says. Six ordinary UPI payments a day leave about ₹50 of spare change that nobody notices and nobody saves. Four friends at ₹50 a day fund an ₹18,000 Goa trip in 90 days.

## Positioning

Jar is gold-first and solo. Chillar is goal-first and social. The brand is the friend who is good with money but never lectures: playful, squad-first and Hinglish. It roasts the habit, never the person.

## Key numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| Hero asset | 55-second Reel, Instagram first |
| Launch | Four weeks, three phases, ₹6 lakh media budget |
| Target | 1,000 squads and 5,500 savers |
| Business model | About ₹93 contribution per saver a year, earned when savings are invested, spent with a partner or boosted with a paid pass |

Saving costs students nothing. Chillar earns on what happens to the money afterwards.
